- On Android, the running timer's notification now shows the task you're working on, your total time today (counting) and the money earned today, on every Android version, instead of the single timer's own clock. The status bar's chip counts today's time too. The money keeps up while TaskMint is closed, and the notification comes back by itself after the phone restarts.
- The Today widget matches it: while a timer runs it shows the task over today's time and the money earned today, and a paused timer says "Paused" with today's time.
- The long-run reminder gives the time as "4h 0m" rather than "04:00", and the Calendar widget's week totals put the money first, like the This week widget.
