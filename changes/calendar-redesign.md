The Calendar is rebuilt. Each day shows what you earned (with `~` on estimates) and how long you worked, a bar
toward your daily goal, and separate markers for money released (or still to be released), withdrawals and the
next withdrawal's opening, with a legend. Weeks have totals, the month has a summary, and days can be reached
and opened from the keyboard. A day's breakdown now compares tracked and reported time, shows release times and
the site's statuses, lists that day's withdrawals, and steps to the previous or next day.
