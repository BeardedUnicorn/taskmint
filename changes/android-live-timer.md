On Android 16 and later, the running timer's notification is a Live Update:
the time also counts in a chip in the status bar, which says "Paused" while
the timer is. On Android 17 it shows today's time and what you've earned today
beside the timer; the money is as of when TaskMint was last open or the timer
last changed. Android's notification settings for TaskMint can turn Live
Updates off.
