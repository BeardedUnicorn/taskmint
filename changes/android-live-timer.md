On Android 17, the running timer's notification is a Live Update: the time
also counts in a chip in the status bar, which says "Paused" while the timer
is, and the notification shows today's time and what you've earned today
beside the timer. The money is as of when TaskMint was last open or the timer
last changed. An Android 16 phone whose system has Live Updates gets the chip
too. On Android 16 and later the notification also shows on the lock screen,
still without a sound: it moved to a new "Running timer" category in the
phone's notification settings, so a change you made to the old one needs
making again. Android's notification settings for TaskMint can turn Live
Updates off.
