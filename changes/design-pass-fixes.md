- A design pass over every page. The same gap between cards on every page (Sessions, Payouts and Settings had wider ones, and Home a double gap); cards no longer carry empty space under their content; the Start Work bar and the active session's panel have the corners every other card has.
- An empty progress bar reads as empty: its track is neutral, where it was a dimmer shade of the bar's own colour and looked full at 0%.
- A dialog's buttons line up with its text on the right, instead of sitting 16px further out.
- The selected task in the Tasks list is a quiet tint with a mint edge rather than a bright green block, and a row's Delete icon is no longer red until you point at it.
- History's bar chart keeps task names level under their bars when they fit (it always slanted them, leaving a blank band), the phone's line chart no longer cuts off its last point, and the summary's figures share one colour.
- The Calendar's month title stays on one line on a phone, its month totals no longer run into each other there, the agenda's goal bars all end at the same place, and a release or withdrawal's amount fits its day in a narrower month.
- Settings: Restore Exactly and Send test notification stay on one line instead of wrapping in a fixed-width button, the Payouts number fields are no longer the full width of the card, the daily goal's switch lines up with its text, and keyboard shortcuts are drawn as keys.
- A phone's session buttons (Pause All, Switch task) stay on one line and the same height on a 360px-wide screen, a long task name no longer stretches one of two side-by-side cards, and a very large amount shrinks to fit its card.
- Home's "If you wait", "Add time" and "See all in History" line up with the edges of the cards around them, and a withdrawal reminder's Remove sits on its line.
- On Payouts on a phone, a month's name and hours are no longer squeezed into a narrow column beside its amount, and the releases list drops the empty indent it kept for a lock when nothing is held.
- Signing in with an email that has no account says so in plain words on a server that reports it as "user not found".
- With larger system text on a phone, the Calendar's month title no longer runs under the next-month arrow, a week's totals and a day's hours stay on one line, and the bottom tabs' labels keep one size instead of the selected one growing into its neighbours.
- A long task name made of one unbroken word no longer pushes Payouts' daily reconciliation table past its card, and an empty page's message takes less of a phone's screen.
