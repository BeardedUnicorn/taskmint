Tapping a notification opens the page it's about: Payouts for a withdrawal reminder or captured work logs, Home for the daily goal and summary, Sessions for a long run or an idle pause. On Android, notifications show the TaskMint leaf instead of a generic "i", and come in two groups, Reminders and Activity, which your phone's notification settings can silence or let pop up separately (Reminders pop up by default). That's also where Android now sets their sound, in place of Settings' "Play a sound" switch, which did nothing there.
