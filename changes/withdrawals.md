- Record the withdrawals you take from the payment site. The Calendar and Payouts show when your next
  withdrawal opens (date and time, from the "Days Between Withdrawals" setting, 3 by default) and how much it
  will be, with what waiting longer would add. The tray menu shows it too. Withdrawals are part of exports and
  backups.
- Payouts' "Available now" becomes "Available to withdraw" once a withdrawal is recorded: the money released
  since your last one.
